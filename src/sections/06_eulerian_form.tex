We have Lemma which connects Eulerian numbers $\eulerian{n}{k}$ with backward
differences of powers $\nabla^{k} t^{m}$
\begin{lemma} [Eulerian Backward differences]
  For non-negative integers $m,k$, and an arbitrary integer $t$
  \label{lem:eulerian-backward-differences}
  \begin{align*}
    \nabla^{k} t^{m} = \sum_{j=0}^{m} \binom{t+j-k}{m-k} \eulerian{m}{j}.
  \end{align*}
  \begin{proof}
    By Worpitzky identity
    $t^{m} = \sum_{k=0}^{m} \binom{t+k}{m} \eulerian{m}{k}$,
    and by binomial recurrence
    $\binom{n+1}{k} = \binom{n}{k} + \binom{n}{k-1}$,
    see~\cite{Worpitzky1883}.
  \end{proof}
\end{lemma}
