Thus, the transition to formula for sums of powers is quite straightforward,
\begin{align*}
  \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m}
  = \sum_{k=0}^{m} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{n} \binom{j-t+k-1}{k} \right] \nabla^{k} t^m.
\end{align*}
Therefore, by setting $r=0$ into Lemma~\eqref{lem:backward-hockey-stick-identity}
yields closed form of ordinary sums of powers
\begin{proposition}[Ordinary sums of powers]
  \label{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $n,m$, and an arbitrary integer $t$
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+k}{k+1} - \binom{k-t}{k+1}
    \right] \nabla^{k} t^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{proposition}
We may see that the basis $\binom{j-t+k-1+r}{k+r}$ for repeatedly
applied Lemma~\eqref{lem:backward-hockey-stick-identity}
was indeed chosen correctly, because we derived closed form for sums of powers
with ease, simply setting parameter for $r$.

From example above, we may observe binomial coefficients of negative upper
index, which may looking unfamiliar.
However, there is no reason to be afraid of, because negative
upper index binomial coefficients are completely deterministic,
because $\binom{-n}{k} = (-1)^{k} \binom{k+n-1}{k}$.
Moreover, binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ is a polynomial
in $n$, thus accepting even fraction values in its upper index.
Therefore, Proposition~\eqref{prop:ordinary-sums-of-powers}
is also a polynomial in $n$.

Formula for double sums of powers could be derivate similarly,
by setting $r=1$ into Lemma~\eqref{lem:backward-hockey-stick-identity}
which yields
\begin{proposition}[Double sums of powers]
  \label{prop:double-sums-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $n,m$, and an arbitrary integer $t$
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{2}{n}{m}
    = \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+k+1}{k+2}
      - \binom{k-t+1}{k+2} \KnuthRFoldSum{0}{n}{0}
      - \binom{k-t+0}{k+1} \KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{0}
    \right] \nabla^{k} t^m.
  \end{align*}
\end{proposition}
Note that we added correction terms $\KnuthRFoldSum{r}{n}{0}$,
which are $\KnuthRFoldSum{0}{n}{0}=1$, and $\KnuthRFoldSum{1}{n}{0}=n$.

Similarly, we obtain closed formula for multifold sums of powers,
by using Lemma~\eqref{lem:backward-hockey-stick-identity} repeatedly.
Thus,
\begin{theorem} [Multifold sums of powers]
  \label{thm:multifold-sum-of-powers}
  For non-negative integers $r,n,m$, and an arbitrary integer $t$
  \begin{align*}
    \KnuthRFoldSum{r}{n}{m}
    =
    \sum_{k=0}^{m}
    \left[
      \binom{n-t+k+r-1}{k+r}
      -
      \sum_{s=0}^{r-1}
      \binom{k-t+r-1-s}{k+r-s} \KnuthRFoldSum{s}{n}{0}
    \right] \nabla^{k} t^{m}.
  \end{align*}
\end{theorem}
